Hanalei Adventure

The easy river. Flat water, taro fields, the green bridge, and Makana Peak. Best first paddle on the island.

7 days a week 2–4 hours Easy Families

Launch near Hanalei Pier and paddle inland through the wildlife refuge, or drift toward the bay for the mountain view. Stop when you want. There is no waterfall hike at the end — that is the point.

You’ll pass

  • Hanalei National Wildlife Refuge and taro fields
  • The 1912 one-lane Hanalei Bridge
  • Rainforest banks further upriver
  • Views of Hanalei Bay and Makana (“Bali Hai”)

Delivery
