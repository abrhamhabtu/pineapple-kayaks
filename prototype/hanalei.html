Hanalei Adventure

The easy river. Flat water, taro fields, the green bridge, and Makana Peak. Best first paddle on the island.

7 days a week 2–4 hours Easy Families

The easy river. Open 7 days. Launch near Hanalei Pier, paddle inland through taro fields, or drift toward the bay for Makana Peak — “Bali Hai.” Flat water. No waterfall hike. The first-timer paddle on the island.

For first-timers

How the day actually works

  1. Book any dayHanalei runs Saturday and Sunday. If Secret Falls is closed, this is the river.
  2. Pickup in Līhuʻe, or we deliver northWe load the car at the Pineapple Store, or drop at Princeville, Hanalei, or Hāʻena so you skip the drive with a boat on the roof.
  3. Launch near the pierCalm, protected water. 2–4 hours, or stay out longer — you set the pace.
  4. Paddle inland or toward the bayWildlife refuge and taro fields upriver. Bridge photos. Honu and native birds if you look. Lunch in town after.

What you will pass

  • Hanalei National Wildlife Refuge — taro, nēnē, ʻalae kea
  • The 1912 one-lane green Hanalei Bridge
  • Rainforest banks further upriver
  • Hanalei Bay and Makana Peak from the water

A loose timeline

  • Launch at the dock near the pier
  • 15–20 min — riverfront houses, calm banks
  • ~45 min — under the green bridge
  • 1 hour+ — refuge, taro, mountain walls

Included

  • Kayak, paddles, life jackets
  • Dry bag, seats, map
  • Roof racks — we load the car

You bring

  • Water, snacks, reef-safe sunscreen
  • Hat, towel, phone pouch
  • Shoes that stay on in the boat
