Secret Falls, without the guesswork

Secret Falls (Uluwehi) sits about three miles up the Wailua. You paddle, then walk a short jungle trail that crosses a stream. It is only open Monday through Friday.

Go early. The water is calmer, the light is better, and you are not stacking up behind every other rental on the island. Bring water shoes with a heel strap — the trail is mud, not a boardwalk.

Pack water and lunch in the dry bag. Red Eye Snack Shack next to our shop does musubi on weekday mornings. Parking at the river is $15, paid there, not to us.
