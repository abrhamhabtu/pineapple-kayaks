Itinerary · 6 min

Both rivers in one weekend

The state keeps Secret Falls closed Saturday and Sunday. If your flight lands on a weekend, a one-day Falls booking will not save you. The 3-day rental will: paddle Hanalei while the falls are shut, then do the Wailua on Friday or Monday with the same boat still on the car.

$150 for three days. Cheaper than three separate rentals, and you only pick up once. Window at the shop is 8am–12pm.

If you land Friday

  • Friday: Secret Falls. Launch early. 4–6 hours.
  • Saturday: beach, or leave the kayak on the car and rest.
  • Sunday: Hanalei River. 2–4 hours, taro fields, pier photos.

If you land Saturday

  • Saturday or Sunday: Hanalei. Easy first paddle, open 7 days.
  • Monday: Secret Falls before you fly, or as the big day of the trip.

Anahola is another easy east-side paddle if you want a third outing and still have the boat. Ask at pickup — we will tell you if the wind is wrong that morning.

Where the kayak sleeps

Leave it on the car when you can. If you take it off, it needs a garage, locked storage, or a fenced yard. Not a beach parking lot overnight, not the hotel porte-cochère. We will walk you through the straps so you are not guessing at 7am.

Pickup and return

The Pineapple Store, 3-4684 Kūhiō Highway, Līhuʻe — next to Kauaʻi Beach Resort. Show up 10–15 minutes early on day one. Return on the third day in the window we put on the confirmation. Delivery is available if you would rather not drive it to the North Shore yourself.

Keep the kayak. Do both rivers.
