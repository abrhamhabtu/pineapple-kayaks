Planning a group day

Four or more paying guests drops the rate to $40 a person on Secret Falls or Hanalei. For 10+, call us — we will stage the boats and set a start time that keeps you out of the crowd.

  • Doubles for kids and first-timers so nobody gets left behind
  • Book early in summer and around holidays
  • One point person pays, everyone else just shows up
  • Delivery to your rental house is an option at the zone rates

Wedding party or family reunion? Do Hanalei the day before the big event — it is the calmer river and everyone looks good in the photos.
