Multi-day

3-Day Weekend Deal

Keep the kayak. Do both rivers without re-booking. Cheaper than three separate days.

$150 · 3 days Pickup 8am–12pm Delivery available

Secret Falls is closed Saturday and Sunday. This package is how weekend arrivals still get the waterfall: paddle Hanalei on the weekend, Falls on Friday or Monday, and keep the boat in between.

A simple itinerary

  • Friday: Secret Falls
  • Saturday: beach, or just rest
  • Sunday: Hanalei River

Or flip it: Hanalei Saturday, Falls Monday. Anahola is another easy east-side option if you want a third paddle.

Storage

Leave it on the car when you can. If you take it off, it needs a garage, locked storage, or a fenced yard. Not a beach parking lot overnight.
