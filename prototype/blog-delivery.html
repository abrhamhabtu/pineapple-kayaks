Local tips · 5 min

We can bring the kayak to you

Most people pick up at the Pineapple Store in Līhuʻe and we strap the boats to the rental car. If you do not want a kayak on the roof — convertible, cruise-day rental, no car at all — we deliver to the hotel or the house.

The rental is still self-guided. Delivery is just the transport. You still get the kayak, paddles, jackets, dry bag, seats, and the map.

Zone rates, each way

  • Kapaʻa and Līhuʻe — $20
  • Poʻipū — $40
  • Princeville — $45
  • Hanalei and Hāʻena — $55

Add it when you book, or text 808-631-8035 with the address. We will give you a window, not a mystery “sometime after lunch.”

When delivery is the better deal

A guided Secret Falls tour is $100–$300 a person partly because they move you. Delivery on a $45 rental is still a fraction of that, even at the Hāʻena rate. A family of four in Princeville paying $45 each plus $45 each way is still well under one guided seat.

Cruise passengers with a short window: call us. We have done same-day drop-offs when the boats are free. Do not assume walk-up — reservation only.

What we need from you

A place we can legally stop. Hotel porte-cochère or the driveway of the vacation rental. Not a no-parking shoulder on the Hanalei one-lane. Someone on site at the window. If you are doing Secret Falls, we can often meet you closer to the Wailua launch instead of dragging boats to the North Shore for a weekday east-side trip — ask.

Add delivery on the booking, or text the address.
