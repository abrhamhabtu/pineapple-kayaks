Family guide · 6 min

Kayaking Kauaʻi with kids

Kids come on both rivers every week. The rule is simple: an adult in the group, and a double kayak for anyone who cannot hold a straight line yet. We fit the life jackets. You set the pace.

Start on Hanalei

Hanalei is the family river. Flat, 2–4 hours, no hike at the end, open Saturday and Sunday. Taro fields, the green bridge, a swim if they want one. If the only day you have is a weekend, this is the one — Secret Falls is Monday–Friday only.

Secret Falls is the bigger day: two miles of paddling plus a muddy trail with a stream crossing. Fine for older kids who like a hike and a waterfall swim. Hard on a toddler, and hard on the adult carrying them.

What we put you in

  • Doubles so a parent and child stay in one boat
  • Life jackets in kid sizes — say ages when you book
  • A dry bag for snacks, phones, and the extra shirt

Water shoes with a heel strap for the Falls trail. Flip-flops come off in the mud. Reef-safe sunscreen, more water than you think, and lunch if you are doing the Wailua. There is no store at the falls.

Ages, honestly

We do not publish a hard minimum because kids vary. A calm 6-year-old in a double on Hanalei is a good day. A restless 4-year-old on a 5-hour Falls trip is not. Call 808-631-8035 and tell us who is coming — we will pick the river and the boats.

Four or more paying guests is $40 a person. A family of four is already on the group rate. One person books; everyone else just shows up.

After the paddle

Red Eye Snack Shack next to the shop does musubi on weekday mornings. On the North Shore, Hanalei town is five minutes from the pier — shave ice, bathrooms, a reset before the drive back.
