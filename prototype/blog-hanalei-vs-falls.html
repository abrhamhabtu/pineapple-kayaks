Hanalei or Secret Falls?

Both are flat, beginner-friendly paddles. The difference is the day you want.

Secret Falls is the waterfall day: two miles up the Wailua, a mile of jungle trail, and a 100-foot fall you can swim under. It takes 4–6 hours and the state keeps it Monday–Friday only. Pick it if the group is up for a muddy hike and wants the payoff photo.

Hanalei is the easy river: taro fields, the one-lane green bridge, Makana (“Bali Hai”) in the distance. No hike at the end, open 7 days, usually 2–4 hours. Pick it for younger kids, anyone who does not want mud, or a Saturday/Sunday.

Weekend on the island and want the falls anyway? Take the 3-day deal, do Hanalei on Saturday, and paddle Secret Falls on Monday before you fly out.
