What to pack for the river

We hand you the kayak, paddle, life jacket, dry bag, seats, map, and roof racks. Here is what is on you.

  • Water shoes or sandals with a heel strap — the Falls trail is mud
  • Water and lunch, sealed in the dry bag we give you
  • Reef-safe sunscreen and a hat
  • Bug spray for the jungle stretch
  • A towel and dry shirt in the car for after
  • Phone in a waterproof pouch if you want photos

The thing everyone forgets: water. It is humid, you are paddling, and there is no store at the falls. One bottle per person is not enough on a hot day.

After: Red Eye Snack Shack next to our shop (Mon–Fri, 7:30–3) for musubi. On the North Shore, Hanalei town has lunch and shave ice five minutes from the pier.
