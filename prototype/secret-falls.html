Secret Falls Adventure

Kayak the Wailua River, hike about a mile, swim under Uluwehi Falls. Self-guided. We set you up.

Mon–Fri 4–6 hours ~4 miles paddling ~2 miles hiking Moderate

Paddle the Wailua through jungle, hike about a mile, swim under a 100-foot fall. Self-guided — nobody is counting minutes. We load the car, hand you the map, and tell you which parking lot. You do the rest.

Good for families, couples, and first-timers with moderate fitness. The river is flat. The hike is mud, not a boardwalk.

For first-timers

How the day actually works

  1. Book a weekdayState rule: Monday–Friday only. Land on a weekend? Use the 3-day deal and do Falls on Friday or Monday.
  2. Pickup in Līhuʻe, or we deliverThe Pineapple Store, next to Kauaʻi Beach Resort — five minutes from the launch. Show up 10–15 minutes early. No car, or no roof? Add delivery at checkout.
  3. We load the car and brief the lotKayak, paddles, jackets, dry bag, map, roof racks. $15 parking at the Wailua, paid to the attendants. Two lots, opposite sides of the river — we tell you which one.
  4. Paddle, hike, swimAbout 2 miles upriver, then a mile of trail each way. Swim under Uluwehi. Eat on the bank. Come back when you are ready. Plan 4–6 hours.

Included

  • Kayak, paddles, life jackets
  • Dry bag, seats, river map
  • Safety brief and launch notes
  • Soft roof racks — we load the car

You bring

  • Water and lunch
  • Reef-safe sunscreen and bug spray

  • Towel, dry shirt, phone pouch
