Secret Falls Adventure

Kayak the Wailua River, hike about a mile, swim under Uluwehi Falls. Self-guided. We set you up.

Mon–Fri 4–6 hours ~4 miles paddling ~2 miles hiking Moderate

This is the classic Kauaʻi day: river, jungle trail, 100-foot waterfall. You go at your own pace with a custom map. Launch early if you want quieter water and softer light.

State rule: Secret Falls is only open Monday through Friday. Weekend travelers should book Hanalei, or take the 3-day deal and do Falls on Friday or Monday.

What’s included

  • Kayak, paddle, and life vest
  • Dry bag and custom river map
  • Soft roof racks — we load the car
  • Local launch and parking notes

What to bring

Water, snacks or lunch, reef-safe sunscreen, bug spray, towel, and water shoes with a heel strap. The trail is muddy. Slippers will fail you. Red Eye Snack Shack (Mon–Fri, 7:30–3) is next door for musubi and sandwiches.

Good to know

  • $15 parking at the Wailua River, paid to the attendants
  • Show up 10–15 minutes early at The Pineapple Store
  • Cruise ship guests: vehicle + pickup available for $50 extra — call us

FareHarbor calendar. Weekdays only — weekends show as unavailable.
