The best kayak price on Kauaʻi

Guided Secret Falls tours on the Wailua usually run $100 to $300 a person. You paddle the same river we put you on. The difference is you do not pay for a guide in the boat, a boxed lunch you did not pick, or a van that waits on the clock.

Our self-guided rentals start at $45 a person. Groups of 4+ are $40. Keep a kayak for three days for $150. That is the cheapest way on Kauaʻi to get to Secret Falls or the Hanalei River without buying your own boat.

What $45 actually includes

The kayak, paddles, life jackets, dry bag, seats, a map to the falls, and soft roof racks. We load the car at the Pineapple Store in Līhuʻe. There is no extra gear fee at the counter.

What is not in the $45: lunch, water, parking at the Wailua ($15, paid there), and hotel delivery if you want it. Those are the only add-ons people actually hit.

How we stay cheaper than a tour

A guided Wailua trip is selling a schedule. You launch when the van launches. You hike when the group hikes. You leave the pool when the guide says so. That costs money to staff.

We hand you the boat and the map. You stop to swim. You eat on the bank. You paddle back when you are ready. One of the owners used to run Kamokila Village on this river — the route is not a mystery. You do not need a guide in the cockpit to find the trail.

  • $45 — Secret Falls (Mon–Fri) or Hanalei (7 days), per person
  • $40 — each, in a group of 4 or more
  • $150 — keep the kayak 3 days, both rivers

When a tour is still the right call

If nobody in the group wants to drive with a kayak on the roof, book our delivery instead of a tour — it is still far cheaper. If you want Hawaiian history narrated the whole way, a guided company is built for that. If you want the waterfall, the swim, and the photo without the $200 seat, rent from us.

How to get the lowest number

Four paying guests on one booking is the $40 rate. A family of five, a couple plus friends, a wedding party — that is the move. Staying a weekend? The 3-day deal is cheaper than three day rentals, and it is how you still see Secret Falls when you land on a Saturday (the state keeps the falls Monday–Friday only).
