Līhuʻe

Local boats. River people.

Pineapple Kayaks is a locally owned kayak and paddleboard shop at The Pineapple Store on Kūhiō Highway — next to Kauaʻi Beach Resort, just before the Hanamāʻulu light. We are about five minutes from the Wailua River launch.

One of the owners previously ran Kamokila Village on the Wailua. That is the legacy we talk about: we know the river, we load the gear, and we would rather send you out self-guided than sell you a packed tour.

What we rent

  • Single and double kayaks
  • SUPs
  • Self-guided Secret Falls and Hanalei trips
  • Guided Wailua eco tours when you want a guide
