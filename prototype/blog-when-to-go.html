Trip guide · 6 min

When to go to Secret Falls

Secret Falls is a weekday trip. The state shuts the landing Saturday and Sunday. If that is your only window, book Hanalei or the 3-day deal and do the falls on Friday or Monday.

Go early

Morning water on the Wailua is calmer. The parking lots fill. The trail is less of a conga line. A Monday morning in the shoulder season is the quiet version of this island. A Wednesday in July at 11am is everyone else’s vacation too.

Plan 4–6 hours door to door: pickup in Līhuʻe, $15 parking at the river, two miles up, the hike, a swim, and the paddle back. Downstream is often the harder half when the afternoon trade winds come up. Leaving the pool at 2pm is how people get tired.

Parking at the Wailua

$15, paid to the attendants, not to us. Two lots on opposite sides of the river. We tell you which one before you leave the shop — do not guess from a blog map you saved last year. Have cash or a card ready so you are not blocking the entrance.

Rain, wind, and when we call it

A Kauaʻi shower is normal. The trail is already mud. Heavy rain overnight is different: the stream crossing on the hike can rise, and we would rather move you to Hanalei or another morning than send you into a bad crossing. We will text if the day should change. You can change the time without a fee; cancel 48 hours out for a full refund.

Hanalei takes afternoon wind on the bay end. Start that one in the morning too if you can.

What to have in the dry bag

Water, lunch, reef-safe sunscreen, bug spray for the jungle stretch, phone in a pouch. Strapped water shoes — the hike is not a boardwalk. Full list is in what to pack.

Monday–Friday. Early is better.
